# Southeast load-census research — checked October 3, 2026

Read-only research: no Site files edited. This archive is a complete extraction of a **defined Georgia public reporting screen**, plus aggregate Duke/TVA/Southern/FPL evidence and 13 named industrial case records. It is **not** a complete census of Southeast customers. Unknown overall denominators remain null.

- `georgia_projects.json`: 118 Q2 2026 Main rows, 80,059.479 MW customer-announced ultimate load. Includes 108 Data Center/Crypto, 6 Manufacturing (1,854.479 MW), 4 Clean Energy Tech (538 MW). Existing Site has exactly the same 118 anonymous records plus a separate named OpenAI record; the earlier “119 anonymous” description is inaccurate. Main MW/use/target fields match all 118 existing records. `existingProjectId` and zero-padded `sourceRowId` support enrichment only, not a stable cross-quarter identity.
- `georgia_changes.json`: all six supplemental change tabs: load6, ramp19, stage1, removed6, initialservice10, added11. Added11/6,776MW already overlap Main. Eight initial dates delayed and two advanced. Removed6/1,382MW must **not** be called cancelled: four/882MW remain active but below the screen; the other two have raw reasons No Decision (450MW) and Site (50MW). Separate `georgia_removed_from_screen.json` preserves raw reasons, `rawStatus:removed_from_screen`, `include:false`. Anonymous change rows cannot be reliably linked to Main by MW.
- `load_observations_additions.json`: five schema-compatible Duke observations. Latest September25 testimony corrects the Spring table to 43 Advanced projects/7.941GW = ESA16/4.332 + Letter24/3.259 + Late3/0.350. **The exact portfolio month is February2026**, stated in April17 report p2; September filing date is not a newer portfolio snapshot. April17 table printed ESA4.337/total7.946, inconsistent with its own narrative and September table. Nearly6GW Developing is separate, site-specific, studies begun; count and precise snapshot date unknown. Forecast-discounted MW is not queue MW. Parent and child observations are non-additive.
- `tva_public_approval_events.json`: three public approval events, not three necessarily unique customers: Nov2024 named xAI150MW, Feb2026 unnamed existing LPC customer additional>100MW, Aug2026 unnamed new direct customer>100MW. All board conditions and earliest requested dates preserved. 2026 identities and detailed terms are in confidential memos; no proof of actual energization in the approvals.
- `named_industrial_additions.json`: 13 assets covering battery, auto, steel, aluminum, semiconductor and hydrogen. Includes owner/utility/state sources and delayed cases Ford/VinFast. Whole-site demand and remaining unenergized MW unknown for all13. Plug40MW and FPL25MW are explicitly electrolyzer equipment ratings, never grid-request MW. No anonymous-to-named match or additive roll-up is authorized by evidence. FPL is a utility-owned pilot, not a separate retail customer.
- `source_inventory.json`: official source universe/date inventory. `coverage.json` gives honest denominators, name/MW unknown fractions within explicitly scoped sets and disclosure gaps.
